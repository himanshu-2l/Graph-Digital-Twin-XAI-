\section{The 3 Non-Negotiable Interview Rules}

\begin{trapbox}{The 3 Rules to Clear the Technical Round}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Rule 1: Always Think Aloud.} The interviewer wants to assess your engineering thought process. Never sit in silence for 30 seconds. Say: \textit{"Here is how I am analyzing this problem: first, let me clarify the constraints..."}
    \item \textbf{Rule 2: Never Fake an Answer.} If asked about a tool you have not used (e.g., a specific proprietary SIEM tool): \textit{"I haven't used that specific tool in production yet, but conceptually I understand it ingests log telemetry like Syslog and NetFlow, which relates to the data ingestion pipelines I built in Python. I am very eager to learn it."}
    \item \textbf{Rule 3: Tie Everything to Evernorth's Mission.} Constantly remind the interviewer: \textit{"In healthcare, security failures risk patient care and sensitive medical records. That's why high precision and automated mitigation are essential."}
\end{enumerate}
\end{trapbox}
