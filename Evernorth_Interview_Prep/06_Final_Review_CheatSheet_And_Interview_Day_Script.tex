\subsection{Script 4: The 4-Step Lifesaver Framework (When You Don't Know the Answer)}
If the interviewer asks a technical question you have never heard before (e.g., an obscure Linux kernel vulnerability or specific Active Directory protocol):

\begin{alertbox}{The 4-Step Lifesaver Protocol (Never Guess Blindly!)}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Step 1: Pause \& Validate (2 seconds):} Take a calm breath. Do not panic or invent false technical terms.
    \item \textbf{Step 2: Anchor to Known Fundamentals:} \textit{``I haven't encountered that specific protocol/tool in my lab work yet, but based on core networking/security principles, here is how I understand its function...''}
    \item \textbf{Step 3: Reason Aloud (First Principles):} Walk through your analytical thought process: \textit{``If I were designing a defense against this, I would check the access controls first, inspect the network logs for anomalies, and isolate the endpoint to prevent lateral movement.''}
    \item \textbf{Step 4: Show Humility \& Drive to Learn:} \textit{``I would verify the official RFC documentation or vendor advisory immediately after the incident. I am very eager to explore this further!''}
\end{enumerate}
\end{alertbox}
