\section{What Evernorth is Facing Right Now (The Threat Landscape \& Challenges)}
\textit{Dropping these insights during your interview will immediately prove you understand their real-world problems:}

\begin{techbox}{Critical Industry Challenges Facing Evernorth}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Ransomware Crisis on Healthcare Infrastructure:}
    In February 2024, the catastrophic ransomware attack on Change Healthcare (a major US clearinghouse) paralyzed prescription billing and claims processing nationwide for weeks. As a dominant pharmacy benefits leader, **Evernorth cannot afford a single minute of downtime**. Cyber resilience and automated attack mitigation are top board-level priorities.
    \item \textbf{API Security \& Healthcare Interoperability:}
    Evernorth connects thousands of hospital systems, independent pharmacies, doctors, and patients. These integrations happen over REST APIs. Attackers constantly attempt credential stuffing, Broken Object Level Authorization (BOLA), and API telemetry tampering.
    \item \textbf{Extreme Class Imbalance in Security Telemetry (Alert Fatigue):}
    Evernorth's cloud networks generate billions of log events daily. SOC analysts are flooded with 40,000+ daily alerts. If detection tools have low precision, analysts suffer from **alert fatigue**, allowing real intrusions to slip past unnoticed. \textit{(Your AML project's 98.2\% precision is a direct solution!)}
    \item \textbf{Stringent Regulatory Compliance (HIPAA, HITECH, HITRUST):}
    A breach of Protected Health Information (PHI) results in massive financial penalties and devastating reputational loss. Security systems must enforce Zero Trust, least privilege, and immutable logging.
\end{enumerate}
\end{techbox}
